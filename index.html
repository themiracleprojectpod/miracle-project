A note from us

We feel called to this.

Dear friend,

Whether this is your first time tuning in or you've been here since the beginning, we're so grateful you're here.

We started The Miracle Project with one belief: what we look for, we find. God loves us, and He is a God of miracles. We sit down with people who have seen His hand in the details of their lives, and we share it with anyone who will listen. While we're asleep, someone on the other side of the world can be touched by something a guest shared months ago.

We're both full-time students who also work. Some weeks Coleton has put more than 40 hours into the podcast, and Gabby has poured in just as much heart. We're so happy to do it. But we don't get paid, we actually pay to do it, and we want it to last so we can bless more people for years, not months.

If you're in a position to give, even a little each month, you'd be helping someone else find their miracle. And if all you can do right now is listen and share, we love that too.

With love,
Coleton & Gabby

Questions before you give

Can I change or cancel my gift?

Yes, anytime. Go to Manage my gift, enter the email you used, and Stripe will send you a link to change your amount, update your card or cancel. Or message us on Instagram and we'll take care of it. No questions asked.

Where does my money go?

Into making the podcast: studio time, equipment, editing, and getting episodes and clips in front of more people. Supporters get a monthly update on what their gifts made possible.

Is my gift tax-deductible?

No. The Miracle Project is a podcast, not a registered nonprofit (yet), so gifts aren't tax-deductible.

Is checkout secure?

Yes. Payments are handled by Stripe, the same processor used by millions of businesses. We never see your card number. Apple Pay and Cash App work too.

Can I give one time instead?

Of course. Give a one-time gift in any amount.

Do I need to make an account?

No. Just your email and payment details at checkout. It takes about a minute.
